Questions, answered.

Everything about the 40% flight discount, how stays are priced, and what to expect from a car rental booked through Elite Orbits.

Flights

It's applied automatically to eligible domestic routes in India, the USA, and Canada when you search — no coupon code needed. The discounted fare is what you see on the route card.

No, the 40% member rate currently covers domestic routes only — within India, within the USA, and within Canada. International routes aren't included yet.

Change and cancellation terms follow the operating airline's fare rules, shown to you before you confirm the booking. Elite Orbits doesn't add extra change fees on top of the airline's own policy.

Stays

Many of our stays come from the same class of independent hosts you'd find on general booking platforms. Because we work directly with a host network, the price shown to you is typically lower than the standard listed rate for a comparable home.

Standard stays can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in. Some host listings may carry a stricter policy, which is always shown on the listing before you book.

Car rentals

Rentals are set up for pickup at the airport you land in, so you can go from arrivals straight to the rental counter or lot.

The base price shown covers the vehicle rental. Insurance options and mileage caps are listed clearly before you reserve, so you can add coverage if you want it.
