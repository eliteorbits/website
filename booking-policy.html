Booking & payment policy

Account required

You need a free Elite Orbits account to complete a booking. This keeps every booking and its 40% member rate tied to a verifiable email address rather than an anonymous session.

Pricing shown at search

The price shown when you search — including the 40% member discount — is the price you pay at checkout, before taxes and any fees charged directly by the airline, host, or rental company. Prices can change between searches if availability changes.

Payment

Payment is processed at the time of booking through our payment provider. Elite Orbits does not store your full card details on its own servers.

Booking confirmation

Once a booking is confirmed, you'll receive a reference number and confirmation details. Flights, stays, and car rentals booked together are grouped under a single itinerary where possible.

Changes after booking

To change dates, passenger details, or cancel a booking, see our Cancellation & Refund Policy or contact support.
