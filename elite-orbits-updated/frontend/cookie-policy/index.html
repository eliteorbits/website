Cookies & local storage policy

What we actually use

Elite Orbits does not currently set tracking or advertising cookies. To keep you signed in, the site stores your login session in your browser's local storage — a standard browser feature similar to a cookie, but readable only by this site and only on the device you're using.

What's stored locally

Your account session token and basic profile info (name and email) are stored in your browser so you don't have to log in again on every page. Clearing your browser's site data, or logging out, removes this information from your device.

Third-party services

If we later add analytics, advertising, or embedded content (such as a Google Reviews widget or a payment provider's checkout), those services may set their own cookies. This page will be updated if that changes.

Your choices

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Doing so will log you out of Elite Orbits on that device.
